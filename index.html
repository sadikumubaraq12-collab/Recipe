Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. the classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese,vegetetable or meats.



Preparation Time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation:5 minutes
  • Cooking:5 minutes

Ingredient

  • 2-3 Eggs
  • Salt,to taste
  • pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional Filling:Cheese, diced vegetables, cooked meats, herbs

Instruction

  1. Beat the Egg: In a bowl, beat the egg with a pinch of salt and pepper until they are well mixed. you can add a tablespoon of water or milk for a fluffier tecture.
  2. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick frypan over medium heat and add oil or butter.
  3. Cook the omelette:Once the butter is melted and bubbling, pour in the eggs.Tilt the pan to ensure the egg evenly coat the surface
  4. Adding filling(optional):When the egg begins to set at the edges but are still slightly runny in the middle, sprinkle your chosen filling ovve one half of the omelette
  5. Fold and serve:As the omelette continue to cook, carefully lift edge and fold it over the filling.Let it cook for another minute, then slide it onto a plate.
  6. Enjoy:Serve hot, with additional salt and pepper if needed
